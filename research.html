Research

Current Research

Our work connects beam diagnosis and accelerator operation, ASIC R&D, AI/ML on front-end electronics, Belle II detector operation, and physics analysis. We develop technologies that can move from today's Belle II systems toward future experiments.

2021-Present

Beam Diagnosis

Fast beam diagnostic systems at SuperKEKB for understanding sudden beam loss events, including BOR and XRM.

The XRM upgrade concept uses multi-channel X-ray sensor readout and RFSoC-based waveform digitization to measure fast beam-size changes.

2026-Present

ASIC R&D

An open-source waveform digitizer ASIC modeled on the IRSX, designed entirely with an open process design kit (GF180MCU) and open-source tools. The full design is public.

Vikas Kumar presented the project at CPAD 2026 as a talk and a poster, and received a poster award.

GitHub CPAD2026 UH News
2024-Present

AI/ML on Front-End Electronics

Running AI/ML directly in detector readout hardware for low-latency decisions. We study FPGA- and RFSoC-based readout, fast-timing digitizers, and hardware-aware machine learning for future experiments.

ML4FE CPAD2024 PHYS476
2020-Present

Accelerator Operation: Background Mitigation and Collimator Studies

Reducing Belle II beam background with collimators at SuperKEKB, from movable-collimator tuning in the main ring to new studies of beam-transport-line collimators for injection background.

2025-Present

Belle II KLM and TOP Operation

Detector operation and hardware support for Belle II KLM and TOP systems, including readout robustness, firmware, high-background operation, and KLM TRG upgrades.

TOP DAQ Preprint TOP NIM-A 2025 NIM-A 2025
Belle II

Physics Analysis: Semileptonic BSM Searches

We are pursuing a direct search for right-handed heavy neutrinos in B->D* ℓ N decays, using semileptonic channels related to the R(D) and R(D*) anomalies. We also plan broader dark-sector searches at Belle II.

Hardware

Electronics, Test Benches, and Readout Systems

  1. Belle II Run Coordination (2020–2023) — Led daily operations and commissioning coordination.
  2. Beam Collimator Optimization (2020–2022) — Reduced beam background by tuning movable collimators.
  3. KLM Operation (2020–2021) — Reduced DAQ errors, implemented diagnostics with Elasticsearch.
  4. LHC-ATLAS SUSY Searches (2014–2020) — Conducted stop/Higgsino searches with ML.
  5. TRT Operation at ATLAS (2016–2019) — Maintained full TRT performance at high luminosity.
  6. Higgs → WW Analysis (2011–2014) — Developed fake-lepton suppression methods contributing to discovery.
